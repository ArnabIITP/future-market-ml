# Third-party data and software

## Nasdaq BX TotalView-ITCH sample data

The multi-session study downloads official sample archives from Nasdaq's BX ITCH public directory. Nasdaq's public directory does not state an open data license. The repository does not redistribute the raw archives or reconstructed event tables; obtain the samples from Nasdaq and follow its applicable data-use terms. The study retains download URLs, file sizes, and local SHA-256 hashes for provenance.

## MeatPy

The optional multi-session ITCH pipeline uses MeatPy 0.5.0 for ITCH parsing and order-book reconstruction. MeatPy is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause License; its license and attribution are included in the installed package. See the [MeatPy project](https://pypi.org/project/meatpy/) and its [JOSS paper](https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.10480).
